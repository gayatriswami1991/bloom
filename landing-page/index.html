FAQ

Questions we hear every day

How much does a hair transplant cost in Mumbai?

It usually ranges from about ₹30,000 to ₹1,50,000 or more, depending on the number of grafts, the technique and the surgeon. After assessing your scalp, we give you a personalised estimate at your consultation.

Is a hair transplant painful?

It is done under local anaesthesia. You may feel mild discomfort during the injection, and some swelling or tenderness for a few days afterwards.

Is the result permanent?

Transplanted follicles come from the donor area, which is genetically resistant to hair loss, so they keep growing. Existing non-transplanted hair may still thin over time.

When will I see results?

Shedding in 2–3 weeks, new growth from 3–4 months, noticeable improvement at 6 months and the full result at 9–12 months.

Who can get a hair transplant?

Usually people with stable pattern hair loss, a healthy donor area and good general health, generally above 21. Only an assessment can confirm suitability.

Can a failed or unnatural transplant be corrected?

Often, yes. Repair can include redesigning the hairline, redistributing grafts and adding density. It depends on your donor reserve and scarring, which we assess first.
