Types of transplant

Which hair transplant is right for you?

Tap a type to see how it works, who it suits and what to expect.

FUE

Follicular Unit Extraction

Individual follicles are taken one by one from the donor area (back and sides of the head) with a micro-punch and placed into the thinning area.

Best suited forMost men and women, short hairstyles, beard and eyebrow work
MarksTiny dot marks that are usually hidden by hair
How it works
  1. Donor hair is trimmed and numbed
  2. Follicles are extracted one at a time
  3. Channels are made and grafts placed at natural angles
Ask about this procedure

DHI

Direct Hair Implantation

Follicles are extracted as in FUE, then placed with a pen-like implanter that sets the depth and direction in a single step.

Best suited forDetailed hairline work and controlled density
MarksTiny dot marks at the donor area
How it works
  1. Follicles extracted
  2. Loaded into the implanter pen
  3. Placed directly at planned depth, angle and density
Ask about this procedure

FUT

Follicular Unit Transplantation (strip)

A thin strip of scalp is removed from the donor area and divided into grafts under magnification, then implanted into the recipient area.

Best suited forCases needing many grafts where hair is worn longer; suitability is decided on examination
MarksA fine linear scar at the donor area
How it works
  1. A narrow donor strip is removed
  2. Grafts are dissected under a microscope
  3. Grafts are placed into the planned area
Ask about this procedure

Beard & Moustache

Facial hair restoration

Follicles are placed into patchy beard or moustache areas at the right angle and direction so facial hair looks natural.

Best suited forPatchy beard, gaps, scars or a thin moustache
MarksUsually minimal; planned per case
How it works
  1. Design the beard line
  2. Extract donor follicles
  3. Implant along natural direction
Ask about this procedure

Eyebrow

Eyebrow reconstruction

Fine grafts are placed one by one at a low angle to recreate the shape, density and direction of the eyebrow.

Best suited forSparse, over-plucked or scarred eyebrows
MarksMinimal
How it works
  1. Shape and arch designed with you
  2. Fine donor follicles selected
  3. Placed at the correct low angle
Ask about this procedure

Female

Female hair transplant

Planned to keep natural hair patterns and add density where thinning shows, often without changing your overall look.

Best suited forWomen with stable, localised thinning and a healthy donor area
MarksMinimal when well planned
How it works
  1. Assess the thinning pattern and cause
  2. Plan the density and parting
  3. Place grafts to blend with existing hair
Ask about this procedure

Repair

Hair transplant repair

Corrects an unnatural hairline, low density, visible scars or poorly placed grafts from an earlier procedure.

Best suited forUnnatural hairline, pluggy look, scarring or poor growth
MarksDepends on previous work; assessed first
How it works
  1. Assess previous work and donor reserve
  2. Redesign the hairline and density
  3. Redistribute or add grafts
Ask about this procedure

FAQ

Questions we hear every day

How much does a hair transplant cost in Mumbai?

It usually ranges from about ₹30,000 to ₹1,50,000 or more, depending on the number of grafts, the technique and the surgeon. After assessing your scalp, we give you a personalised estimate.

Is a hair transplant painful?

It is done under local anaesthesia. You may feel mild discomfort during the injection, and some swelling or tenderness for a few days.

Is the result permanent?

Transplanted follicles come from the donor area, which resists hair loss, so they keep growing. Existing non-transplanted hair may still thin over time.

When will I see results?

Shedding at 2–3 weeks, new growth from 3–4 months, noticeable improvement at 6 months and the full result at 9–12 months.

Who can get a hair transplant?

Usually people with stable pattern hair loss, a healthy donor area and good general health, generally above 21. Only an assessment can confirm suitability.

Can a failed transplant be corrected?

Often, yes. Repair can include redesigning the hairline, redistributing grafts and adding density, depending on your donor reserve and scarring.
